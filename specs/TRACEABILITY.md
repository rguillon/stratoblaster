# Traceability: old specs → new specs

> Generated on 2026-11-01 when the specs were reorganised by function. The old, phase-based
> specs are in [`../specs_old/`](../specs_old/), unchanged.

- **§1** maps every old requirement (`GD-xx`, `ENG-xx`, `Pxx-Ryy`) to the requirements that
  carry it now. `ENG` IDs kept their numbers; the others got new IDs (see [README](README.md)).
- **§2** maps every decision of the old log (D-001 to D-233, in
  [`../specs_old/DECISIONS.md`](../specs_old/DECISIONS.md)) to the requirements that now hold it.
- **§3** is the trace audit made just before the rewrite: requirements that were not traced in the
  code, and why.

Statuses — old requirements: `mapped` (one new requirement), `split` (several), `partial` (this
area holds part of it; the other rows hold the rest), `obsolete` (the feature was removed or the
rule was a one-off). Decisions: `covered` (already a requirement), `new` (a requirement made from
it), `updated` (it changed a requirement), `generalised` (an implementation choice turned into a
general rule), `partial`, `superseded` (by a later decision), `obsolete` (history only).
